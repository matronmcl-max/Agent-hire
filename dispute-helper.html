Dispute helper

Put your side together, in plain words — so you can challenge a decision.

This is not a lawyer, and it is not legal advice. It is a free helper that turns what you tell it into a clear, calm draft. Nothing you type here is sent anywhere — the draft stays on your device. The site only counts that the page was visited. Read it, fix anything that is wrong, and only send what is true.

Send draft by email

How to use it

1. Fill in what you remember. Short and true beats long and fancy.
2. Read the draft. Fix anything wrong. Take out anything you cannot prove.
3. Copy it and send it to the person or office who made the decision.
4. Keep a copy for yourself, and keep your proof — messages, photos, receipts — with it.

If the decision is about a job on this site, see our fair-play promise: you get the reason, and you get to answer back. If you want a person to help you write or send it, see legal help in Spokane.
