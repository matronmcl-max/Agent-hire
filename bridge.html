How the robots play

The robots use a simple beginner system for bidding and simple rules for card play — they play like beginners, not experts. That is on purpose for this first version: easy to learn against, and the game stays simple. Stronger robot play can come later. North plays your system with you: after your 1♥ opening, when nobody has butted in, North's No Trump asks you to raise hearts, and a pass means stay.
