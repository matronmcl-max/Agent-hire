Need work done?

Post the job. A verified club member does it. You only pay when it's done right.

1Post it

Tell us what you need and what you'll pay. Your payment is held safely until the work is approved — never sent into the void.

2A member does it

Someone from the club claims it and gets it done — shelf photos, property checks, local errands, whatever you need on the ground.

3You approve, they get paid

Check the work. If it's right, the money releases to them. The club keeps a flat 7% — out in the open, no hidden cuts.

Gig ideas

Not sure what to post? These are suggestions — post one like any other job, or anything else you need.

Elder day companion

Hang out with an elder for the day — cook breakfast, lunch, or dinner, tidy the house, rides to appointments or the pharmacy.

Fall yard clean up

Yard waste into the bin or bags, leaves, branches, general tidy.

Junk haul & moving

Junk to haul, stuff to move, furniture to build.

Meal prep

Meals prepped for the week, cooked at home.

Local errands

Property checks, shelf photos, pickups, drop-offs.

Learn a skill

Want skills for a line of work? An expert gets paid to teach you — for example, a mechanic shows you how to install an alternator.

Local escorts

Need company for dinner, an event, or a night out? Hire a local male or female escort for companionship.

Massages

Relaxation, deep tissue, or therapeutic massage from a local practitioner, at your place or theirs.

Skydiving instructions

Want to learn to skydive? A licensed instructor walks you through safety, gear, and your first jump.

Flight instructions

Want to learn to fly? A licensed flight instructor teaches you the basics, in the air and on the ground.

Scuba instructions

Want to learn to scuba dive? A certified instructor teaches you safety, gear, and your first dives.

Skateboard teaching

Want to learn to skateboard? A local skater teaches you basics, tricks, and safety.

Bicycle training

Learn to ride, or ride better — a local cyclist trains you on balance, skills, and road confidence.

Bicycle maintenance

Bike need work? A local mechanic tunes, repairs, and maintains your bicycle.

Hunting guides

Heading out hunting? A local guide takes you out, shows you the area, and helps you hunt safe.

Fishing charters

A local charter takes you out fishing, shows you the good spots and techniques, then cleans, prepares, cooks, and serves the fish.

Kayak rentals

Rent a kayak for the day and paddle local lakes and rivers, gear included.

Dirt bikes

Hit the trails on a dirt bike — rentals, guided rides, or lessons from a local rider.

ATV travel

ATV tours and trail rides — explore the backcountry on an ATV with a local guide.
