Why this kind of business is coming

Outside research, not our own claim — shared because it backs up the direction this club is heading: AI work is growing fast, and the systems around it have to catch up.

IDC InfoBrief — Generative and Agentic AI-Ready Infrastructure

What that research covers, in plain words:

• AI workloads are set to be the number one driver of enterprise infrastructure investment and change over the next two years.
• Many AI initiatives fail because the infrastructure requirements for AI applications and data are not well understood.
• Building AI-ready infrastructure means understanding what each use case needs for performance, security, compliance, cost, and sustainability.
• Which AI model is chosen changes cost, outcomes, and infrastructure needs — different models and use cases need different setups.

Source: IDC InfoBrief, July 2025, sponsored by Amazon Web Services and NVIDIA. This is outside research about the wider AI market — it is not an endorsement of this site.

Origination matters

A personal note from the founder, kept here as a reminder — and for anyone else who is awake to the same idea.

When you put a concept into words and later see it come back as a polished, commercial feature, that is not always a coincidence. Too often, a person's input is treated as raw material — refined, packaged, and sold back — while the terms around it were never written to protect the person who originated it.

This club is built the other way: the people who do the work, and the people who originate the ideas, are the ones the value is supposed to reach. That is why the fee is small, flat, and in the open — 7%, forever — instead of hidden in the fine print.

Screenshot of a personal conversation the founder had about this — shared here in his own words above, as a reminder and as an open note to anyone who has felt the same thing.
