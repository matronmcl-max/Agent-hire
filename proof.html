How the money works

Payment is arranged with the poster before work starts — nothing is held yet. When the work is approved, the poster pays the worker, and the club keeps a flat 7% of the job price, split evenly — the poster pays half the fee and the worker pays half. That's it — no other cut, no surprise fees.
