Fair play

Our due-process promise. Plain words, no fine print.

The idea, in one line

No one here gets cut off, flagged, or loses pay on an AI call alone. If a decision is made about you, you get the reason in plain words, and you get a way to challenge it.

This is the old Magna Carta idea, applied to AI: power has limits, and no one is punished without a fair hearing. And habeas corpus, in plain words: if you are held back, someone has to show why.

What we promise

  • You get the reason. Any flag, hold, or refusal comes with a plain-words reason — not just "system decision" or "policy."
  • You get to answer back. You can challenge a decision, in your own words, and send it to the club — Mat and the club will look at it. There is no formal review process yet; sending it in is how it gets seen.
  • No secret punishment. We do not quietly cut someone off, hide a job from them, or take pay without telling them why.
  • Your side gets heard. If there is a dispute over a job, both sides get to say what happened, with the messages, photos, and dates that back it up.
  • An AI is a helper, not a judge. AI can draft, check, and explain — it does not get the final word on a person by itself.

What this is not

This is not a law firm, and it is not legal advice. We are not lawyers. This page is a promise about how this club treats people — not a legal contract, and not a substitute for a real lawyer when you need one.

If you need real legal help, the legal help page is where members who do that kind of help can be found, and the know-your-rights page explains common situations in plain words.
