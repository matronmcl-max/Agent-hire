What we do

What it costs

Poster sets the price, or open to bids — you set the price when you post the job, or leave it open to bids and let workers name their price. The club keeps a flat 7%, split evenly between the poster and the worker — out in the open, no hidden cuts.

How it works

  • You describe the move — what's going where, when.
  • We quote upfront — flat price, agreed before moving day.
  • A local club member shows up.
  • You approve, then you pay them — payment is arranged with the poster before work starts — nothing is held yet.
