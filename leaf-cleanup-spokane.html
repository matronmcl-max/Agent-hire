Leaf Cleanup in Spokane, WA

Leaves piling up? A local will rake them, blow them out of the beds and corners, and bag them up.

What it is

A local club member comes to your yard and clears the leaves — front and back lawn, walkways and driveway blown clear, leaves bagged for pickup. Rakes and bags are provided.

  • Raking — front and back lawns, any size
  • Blowing leaves out of flower beds, corners, and paths
  • Bagging — leaves bagged up and left ready for pickup
  • One-off cleanup or repeat visits through the fall, your call

When you need it

Fall, usually October through November in Spokane, when the leaves are down and thick. A yard left under leaves all winter smothers the grass underneath — getting them up before the first hard freeze matters.

What it costs

Usually $15 an hour, or a flat price if it is a big yard. You set the price when you post the job — or leave it open to bids and let workers name their price. A worker can also counter-offer on a fixed-price job.

How it works

  • You describe the job — how big the yard is, front and back, and when you need it done.
  • You set the price, or open it to bids — flat price, agreed before the job starts, or workers bid and you pick one.
  • A club member does it — local, with rakes and bags provided.
  • You approve, then you pay them — payment is arranged with the poster before work starts — nothing is held yet.

The club keeps a flat 7%, split evenly between the poster and the worker — out in the open, no hidden cuts.
